BIKASH SUNA - PORTFOLIO VIDEOS FOLDER
=========================================================================

Put your video files (.mp4, .webm, .mov) into the matching folders:

📁 short-reels/   -> Fast 9:16 vertical reels, speed ramps, beat-syncs
📁 long-videos/   -> Cinematic albums, weddings, music videos, documentaries
📁 brand-collabs/ -> Paid promotion reels, sponsored reviews, brand collaborations

HOW TO LINK IN YOUR WEBSITE:
Open "src/data/projects.js" and set:
videoUrl: 'assets/videos/short-reels/your_file_name.mp4'
