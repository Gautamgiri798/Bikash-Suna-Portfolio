# Brag Plan: Bikash Suna Portfolio & Media Kit

## What is this app?
The personal portfolio, creator media kit, and interactive booking platform for Bikash Suna — a professional video editor and digital content creator specializing in cinematic Reels, long video albums, and brand collaborations.

## The angle
"Two Passions. One Powerful Creator." Highlighting the unique 2-in-1 synergy of a creator who writes, shoots, edits at 4K 60FPS in professional NLE software, and directly publishes to a verified, engaged audience. Presented as an ultra-premium tech product launch with high-end camera moves, neon laser glows, live NLE timeline interface, and dynamic metric counters.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A cinematic lens aperture reveal with 3D starfield particles, camera viewfinder HUD brackets, and bold typography stating: "BIKASH SUNA — VIDEO EDITOR & CONTENT CREATOR", punctuated by a resonant audio swell and live status pulse.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Act 2 (4.5s – 9.5s):** The Live Master NLE Editing Timeline — zooming in on ProRes 422 HQ 4K timeline deck with V1/V2/A1 tracks, speed ramps, 3D LUT grading, and live dialogue audio waveforms.
- **Act 3 (9.5s – 14.5s):** The Dual Superpowers — smooth split-screen kinetic cards contrasting "The Video Editor" (Velocity curves, kinetic typography, multi-track sound) with "The Content Creator" (Sponsored Reels, product showcases, link stickers, 50K+ reach).
- **Act 4 (14.5s – 19.5s):** Creator Media Kit & Verified Metrics — interactive metric cards ticking up in real-time: 50K+ Monthly Impressions (+32% MoM), 15K–45K Reel Views, 8.4% Engagement Rate (3.2x Average), and 4-Step Campaign Lifecycle.
- **Act 5 (19.5s – 25.0s):** Interactive Pricing & Project Calculator — simulated user interaction selecting project formats (Short Reel Edit ₹600 / Long Video Album ₹3000 / Brand Collab DM), toggling Express Delivery & SFX Suite with animated cost recalculation.

## Outro / punchline
"Ready to Elevate Your Content & Brand?" A glowing holographic sign-off displaying the 48h turnaround guarantee, verified creator badge, and direct WhatsApp concierge booking button.

## User flow worth showing
1. Landing on the hero & inspecting the live NLE timeline editor
2. Exploring the dual service pillars (Post-production + Influencer marketing)
3. Reviewing verified ROI performance metrics in the Media Kit
4. Interactively calculating project deliverables in the live estimator
5. Direct one-click booking through the WhatsApp concierge
